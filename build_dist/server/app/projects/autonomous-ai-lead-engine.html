Autonomous AI-Powered Lead Generation & Enrichment Engine

An AI-powered business lead generation and enrichment platform that automates prospect discovery, extracts structured business data, evaluates prospects using Google Gemini AI, and organizes qualified sales leads through Google Sheets and an interactive analytics dashboard.

Technologies Used

The Business Problem

Sales and marketing teams spend countless hours manually researching company websites, copying contact emails into spreadsheets, and subjectively scoring leads.

The Engineering Solution

Built a fully autonomous pipeline that crawls target domains, utilizes Google Gemini to assess company offerings against ideal customer profiles, enriches contact intelligence, and syncs directly into actionable CRM sheets.

Verified Implemented Features

Automated web business data extraction and contact scraping
AI-powered lead qualification and ICP scoring via Google Gemini
Intelligent prospect filtering and revenue potential estimation
Real-time two-way Google Sheets CRM synchronization
High-concurrency FastAPI backend with asynchronous task execution
Interactive React & Next.js sales analytics dashboard
Resilient API caching and exponential backoff fallback mechanisms
Automated strategic business prospect insights extraction
System Architecture
Step 01Web Data Extraction (BeautifulSoup4 & Python Scraper)

Step 03Data Synchronization (Google Sheets API via gspread)
Step 04API & Automation Layer (FastAPI Asynchronous Backend)
Step 05Presentation Layer (React Analytics Dashboard & Lead Viewer)
